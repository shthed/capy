Capy Paint — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Package: com.capypaint.capy

Capy Paint is a relaxing color-by-number and free-coloring puzzle application available on Web and Android. Your artwork, photos, and puzzle saves belong to you.

1. Local-First Photo & Puzzle Processing

When you choose a personal photo from your device (using the Android system Photo Picker or your browser file selector), color quantization and region segmentation run locally on your device inside a sandboxed Web Worker. Your personal photos are stored locally on your device (IndexedDB / app-private storage) and are never uploaded to external servers unless you explicitly export or share a file.

2. Optional Sign-In & Cloud Save Sync

You can play Capy Paint completely offline without creating an account. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services or enable Cloud Sync, Capy Paint stores compact puzzle completion progress (region completion bitsets and timestamps) so your progress merges safely across your devices. You may sign out or delete your local and cloud saves at any time from the Saves menu.

3. Shareable Links & Optional Co-op Rooms

When you generate a Share Progress Link for a gallery puzzle, completion progress is encoded directly into the URL fragment (#s=...). When you host or join a live Co-op room, only your chosen display name, avatar color, and region paint events are shared with participants in that room code.

4. Optional Rewarded Credits & Zero Forced Tracking

Capy Paint never displays forced interstitial advertisements during gameplay. On supported mobile builds where optional rewarded video credits are offered to fund AI-generated gallery puzzles, ads are strictly opt-in and respect your regional consent preferences. Web and desktop builds are 100% ad-free.

5. Google Play Data Safety Summary

  • Personal Photos & Media: Processed 100% on-device; never collected or shared automatically.
  • Game Progress & App Activity: Stored locally by default; synced via encrypted HTTPS only when you opt in to Google Play Games Services or Cloud Sync.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can play without an account and clear all local or cloud saves directly inside Settings → Saves.

6. Contact & Open Source

Questions or data deletion requests can be submitted via the project repository at https://github.com/shthed/capy.
