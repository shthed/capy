Capy Paint — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Package: com.capyboop.paint

Capy Paint is a relaxing color-by-number and free-coloring puzzle application available on Web and Android. Your artwork, photos, and puzzle saves belong to you.

1. Local-First Photo & Puzzle Processing

When you choose a personal photo from your device (using the Android system Photo Picker or your browser file selector), color quantization and region segmentation run 100% locally on your device inside a sandboxed Web Worker. By default, your personal photos and puzzle saves stay strictly on your device (IndexedDB and app-private storage) and are never sent to Capy Paint servers. If you opt in to Cloud Sync, custom photo puzzle archives (.capy) are backed up solely to your own chosen cloud storage provider (such as Google Drive appDataFolder, Microsoft OneDrive, Apple iCloud / WebDAV, or Google Play Games Saved Games as configured) so you can continue coloring across your own devices. If you explicitly trigger optional Infinite Zoom AI Artwork, only the cropped artwork template and prompt you submit are sent to the AI provider you selected (see Section 5).

2. Optional Sign-In & BYOC Cloud Save Sync

You can play Capy Paint completely offline without creating an account. If you choose to opt in to Bring-Your-Own-Cloud (BYOC) Save Sync — such as Google Drive (via Google Identity Services OAuth 2.0 using openid, profile, email, and the app-isolated drive.appdata scope), Microsoft OneDrive, Apple iCloud / WebDAV (as configured), or Google Play Games Services v2 on Android — Capy Paint reads your basic display profile (name, avatar, and account identifier where applicable) and syncs your save library manifest and compact .capy v2 puzzle saves exclusively inside your app-private cloud folder. Capy Paint cannot view or access unrelated files in your cloud storage. You may sign out or delete your local and cloud saves at any time from Settings → Saves.

3. Optional Anonymous In-Game Feedback

Capy Paint includes an optional Send Feedback form under About. Submitting feedback is completely voluntary and anonymous — no account, email address, or personal identifier is required. When you choose to submit a bug report or feature idea, only your selected category, title, message text, app version, and platform (Web or Android) are sent over HTTPS to our feedback relay to create a public issue on the Capy Paint issue tracker. Do not include personal or sensitive information in feedback submissions.

4. Shareable Links & Optional Co-op Rooms

When you generate a Share Progress Link for a gallery puzzle, completion progress is encoded directly into the URL fragment (#s=...). When you host or join a live Co-op room, only your chosen display name, avatar color, and region paint events are shared with participants in that room code.

5. Optional Infinite Zoom AI Artwork

Capy Paint offers an optional, user-initiated Infinite Zoom feature powered by Google Nano Banana 2.1 / 2 image models. Nothing is sent unless you explicitly start an Infinite Zoom infill or border extension, and Capy Paint does not operate a proxy server or retain your prompts or generated images:

  • Web-Only Puter.js (Web browsers only): On the web version, selecting Puter.js lazily loads https://js.puter.com/v2/ only when you trigger a generation and sends your cropped template image and optional prompt directly to Puter's service under your Puter account. Puter.js is never loaded or included in the Android app.
  • Free Guided Gemini App Handoff (Web & Android): Saves or shares your prepared template image and copies the prompt so you can run it directly in Google's free Gemini app or at gemini.google.com, then paste, pick, or share the resulting image back into Capy Paint for local segmentation.
  • Optional Direct Gemini API Key (BYOK — Web & Android): If you choose to enter your own Google AI Studio API key, the key is stored strictly locally on your device in browser/app storage (capy_gemini_byok_key_v1), is never sent to Capy Paint servers, and is never included in .capy exports or cloud backups. Requests containing your template image and prompt go directly from your device over encrypted HTTPS to Google's Gemini API (https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com) using your key. You can clear your saved key at any time from Menu → Create.

6. 100% Ad-Free & Zero Tracking SDKs

Capy Paint is 100% ad-free across Web and Android, with zero third-party advertising or behavioral tracking SDKs bundled. Gameplay, custom photo segmentation, and local saves run completely on-device without forced telemetry or ads.

7. Google Play Data Safety & Account Summary

  • Personal Photos & Media: Processed 100% on-device; stored locally by default and backed up only to your own private BYOC cloud folder (Google Drive appDataFolder, Microsoft OneDrive, Apple iCloud / WebDAV, or Play Games Saved Games) if you opt in to Cloud Sync.
  • Optional Infinite Zoom AI Artwork: Strictly user-initiated; on Android, templates leave your device only when you manually share them to Google's Gemini app or explicitly configure your own device-local Google AI Studio API key to call generativelanguage.googleapis.com directly over encrypted HTTPS (Capy Paint never receives or stores your key or artwork).
  • Optional In-Game Feedback: Voluntary, user-initiated text submissions sent anonymously over encrypted HTTPS along with app version and platform only; not linked to any personal identity.
  • Account Profile & Game Progress: Stored locally by default; synced via encrypted HTTPS only when you explicitly connect an optional cloud provider.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can play without an account, sign out at any time, and delete local saves from Settings → Saves or revoke app access from your cloud provider's account settings.

8. Contact & Open Source

Capy Paint is playable on the web at https://capypaint.com and https://paint.capyboop.com. Questions, bug reports, or data deletion requests can be submitted via the public issue tracker at https://github.com/capyboop/paint/issues.
