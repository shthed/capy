Capy Paint — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Package: com.capypaint.capy

Capy Paint is a relaxing color-by-number and free-coloring puzzle application available on Web and Android. Your artwork, photos, and puzzle saves belong to you.

1. Local-First Photo & Puzzle Processing

When you choose a personal photo from your device (using the Android system Photo Picker or your browser file selector), color quantization and region segmentation run 100% locally on your device inside a sandboxed Web Worker. By default, your personal photos and puzzle saves stay strictly on your device (IndexedDB and app-private storage) and are never sent to Capy Paint servers. If you opt in to Cloud Sync, custom photo puzzle archives (.capy) are backed up solely to your own private Google Drive appDataFolder or Google Play Games Saved Games storage so you can continue coloring across your own devices.

2. Optional Sign-In & Cloud Save Sync

You can play Capy Paint completely offline without creating an account. If you choose to sign in with your Google Account on Web/PWA (via Google Identity Services OAuth 2.0 using openid, profile, email, and the app-isolated drive.appdata scope) or with Google Play Games Services v2 on Android, Capy Paint reads your basic display profile (name, avatar, and account identifier) and syncs your save library manifest and compact .capy v2 puzzle saves exclusively inside your hidden, app-private cloud folder (Google Drive appDataFolder or Play Games SnapshotsClient). Capy Paint cannot view or access any other files in your Google Drive. You may sign out or delete your local and cloud saves at any time from Settings → Saves.

3. Shareable Links & Optional Co-op Rooms

When you generate a Share Progress Link for a gallery puzzle, completion progress is encoded directly into the URL fragment (#s=...). When you host or join a live Co-op room, only your chosen display name, avatar color, and region paint events are shared with participants in that room code.

4. Optional Rewarded Credits & Zero Forced Tracking

Capy Paint never displays forced interstitial advertisements during gameplay. On supported mobile builds where optional rewarded video credits are offered to fund AI-generated gallery puzzles, ads are strictly opt-in and respect your regional consent preferences. Web and desktop builds are 100% ad-free.

5. Google Play Data Safety & Google Auth Summary

  • Personal Photos & Media: Processed 100% on-device; stored locally by default and backed up only to your own private Google Drive appDataFolder / Play Games Saved Games when you opt in to Cloud Sync.
  • Account Profile & Game Progress: Stored locally by default; synced via encrypted HTTPS only when you sign in with Google Identity Services (drive.appdata) or Google Play Games Services v2.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can play without an account, sign out at any time, and delete local saves from Settings → Saves or revoke app access from your Google Account settings.

6. Contact & Open Source

Questions or data deletion requests can be submitted via the project repository at https://github.com/shthed/capy.
