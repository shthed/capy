## Getting Help or Reporting an Issue

- **In-App Anonymous Feedback**: Open **Settings -> About -> Send Feedback** inside Capy Paint to send a bug report or idea directly without creating an account.
- **Bugs, Crashes, or Visual Glitches**: Open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/capyboop/paint/issues/new/choose) and include your browser or Android device model, app version, and steps to reproduce.
- **Feature Requests and Puzzle Ideas**: Submit a [Feature Request or Puzzle Idea](https://github.com/capyboop/paint/issues/new/choose).
- **Existing Issues**: Browse or search [open issues](https://github.com/capyboop/paint/issues) before creating a duplicate report.

## Quick Troubleshooting

- **Low Browser Storage or Quota Warnings**: Open **Settings -> Saves -> Browser Storage** to view your cache/save usage and run one-tap targeted cleanup without losing your finished puzzles.
- **Back Up or Move Your Puzzles**: Export your library as a portable `.capy` archive in **Settings -> Saves**, or copy a **Share Progress** link for any single puzzle.
- **Replay the Interactive Guide**: Tap the **`?`** button in the top-right corner of the **Settings** modal anytime to revisit controls, hints, and accessibility tools.
- **Easier Tapping on Small Shapes**: Adjust **Text Size**, **UI Size**, and **Fat-Finger Tap Assist** reach under **Settings -> Accessibility**.

## Offline and Privacy Notes

- All photo-to-puzzle processing runs locally on your device inside a sandboxed Web Worker. Your photos are never uploaded to a server for puzzle generation.
- Optional Bring-Your-Own-Cloud Sync (Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Apple CloudKit, or Android Google Play Games Services) and Co-op Rooms are strictly opt-in (currently gated off by default).
- If you encounter a security vulnerability, please follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
